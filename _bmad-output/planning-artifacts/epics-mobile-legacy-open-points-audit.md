# Mobile open points checked against the legacy application

## Method and evidence boundary

This audit reviews the legacy Flutter checkout at sibling repository `tellpal`, revision `e0c31b8`, together with the user-supplied iPhone screenshots and decisions already recorded in `epics-mobile.md`. The only uncommitted legacy change visible during the audit was an Xcode project file; this audit did not edit the legacy checkout. Code establishes implemented branches and intended navigation; it does not prove current Remote Config values, deployed backend responses, native store outcomes or physical-device behavior. Replacement acceptance must preserve the approved product decisions even where they supersede the legacy code.

**Resolution labels:** `Reference fixed` means the legacy client or the user's device observation settles the user-visible behavior for planning. `Contract pending` means backend/provider/device evidence is still needed during implementation. `Product question` means the replacement plan contains behavior not demonstrated by the legacy app and the product owner must choose its scope.

## Product decisions — 2026-10-01

1. Notification/deep-link destination routing is a **new replacement feature**. Story 4.5 needs a new provider/route contract and Android/iOS acceptance evidence; it is not legacy parity.
2. Portuguese announcements **do not use English artwork as fallback**. Use Portuguese artwork when available; otherwise omit the unavailable announcement without a broken card.
3. When a later device carries conflicting old local records, **existing new-system account records remain authoritative**. Do not overwrite or automatically merge over them. Unique local records still need a source/target fixture and safe disposition; the already approved new-system-only account-deletion scope is unchanged.
4. CMS manages **new-language** tester-to-live promotion after checks. It does not operate mobile app version rollout or old-system shutdown.
5. The user personally approves and operates staged **mobile app version** release in the store consoles. This assigns decision ownership, not cohort sizes, thresholds or a present authorization to publish.

## Remaining decisions and evidence

OQ-10 still needs stages, observation periods and stop/expand thresholds for the **mobile store release**, plus a separate legacy-system shutdown decision. The user is the mobile release approval/operator owner. OQ-3 still needs field-level treatment of records unique to a later device. OQ-6 deletion/remigration mechanics, OQ-9 provider/route formats and the other gates above require contracts and executed evidence rather than further assumptions from legacy source.

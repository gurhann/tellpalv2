topic: Shared ownership of textless covers
updated: 2026-09-07T09:56
---

- (direction) Story 1.3 applies shared textless-cover ownership after localized story narration support.
- (decision) A textless cover is one shared IMAGE asset owned by Content for STORY audio experience, LULLABY, and MEDITATION; normal STORY title-bearing covers remain localization-scoped.
- (constraint) The content module stores only a positive IMAGE asset reference; asset URLs, variants, and processing outputs remain owned and resolved by the asset module.
- (constraint) Changing a shared textless cover must not modify any localization-specific cover, narration, audio, title, description, or publication state.
- (constraint) A shared textless cover is not language-specific and must be returned consistently in every localization projection for its content.
- (constraint) AUDIO_STORY canonical-type removal, legacy content import, public/mobile discovery contract changes, and shared lullaby playback are out of scope.
- (assumption) Existing content-level textlessCoverMediaId is retained as the persistence ownership point rather than adding a duplicate media field.
- (capability) CAP-1: An editor can set or clear one optional shared textless cover on eligible canonical content so cross-language media is managed once.
- (capability) CAP-2: Every localization read model can identify the same shared cover while preserving its own localized cover and other fields.
- (capability) CAP-3: CMS presents the shared-cover editor at content scope for supported types and does not duplicate it in localization editing.
- (decision) The shared cover is optional and its absence does not block existing publication or trigger automatic asset processing; later playback and import stories may make use of it.
- (event) Coherence validation passed: three stable capabilities have testable intent and success; constraints, non-goals, and a concrete success signal are represented in the derived contract.
- (event) Preservation validation passed: all load-bearing epic decisions on cover scope, localization isolation, asset ownership, and deferred work are captured; wrapper-only discussion was omitted.
- (decision) Supersedes the earlier single-cover interpretation: Content.textlessCoverMediaId is the existing STORY source cover containing the textless equivalent of the localized title-bearing cover; it must never serve as the audio-story listening cover.
- (decision) A distinct content-scoped listening-experience cover reference is required for STORY narration, MEDITATION, and LULLABY. It is one shared positive IMAGE asset across all localizations.
- (constraint) A STORY has three separate image concepts: content-level source textless cover, localization-level title-bearing reading cover, and content-level listening-experience cover. Updating one cannot overwrite either other concept.
- (event) Coherence validation passed after correction: CAP-1 through CAP-3 preserve distinct source, localized reading, and listening-cover responsibilities.
- (event) Preservation validation passed after correction: the user-defined second STORY textless cover is captured as a separate content-scoped listening-cover reference, not as a reinterpretation of the existing source field.
- (event) Upstream epic-1-context.md was synchronized with the corrected distinction between STORY source cover, localized reading cover, and separate listening-experience cover.
